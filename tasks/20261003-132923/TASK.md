Calibrated slider ranges and stroke spacing:
- Border thickness range calibrated to 1.0–6.0px (default 2.0px).
- Dash / dot gap range calibrated to 2.0–16.0px (default 5.0px).
- Dotted strokes use (diameter + gap) step distance to keep dots distinct.
- Dashed strokes avoid duplicate overlapping endpoint circles that closed gaps.
